The permissive license for the tie ecosystem — and beyond

The Tie Public License (TPL) is a permissive, attribution-based open source license. It is general-purpose and field-of-use neutral: anyone may adopt it for any kind of work, and anyone may use licensed works for any purpose.

TPL is published and maintained by the TIE-LANG organization. No affiliation with, or endorsement by, the organization is required to use it.

Why TPL

  • Universal grant. Use, copy, modify, and redistribute — for any purpose, commercial or non-commercial, in any field of endeavor.
  • Attribution respected. Notice and attribution obligations protect every rightsholder in the distribution chain (Section 4).
  • User Works stay yours. Works you author for or with the Licensed Material are your own — no copyleft, no required notices (Section 6).
  • AI terms made explicit. Training a model on Licensed Material is permitted use; model weights and outputs are not Licensed Material (Section 6.4).
  • Third-party assets pass through. Licenses and notices accompanying third-party material are preserved and conveyed (Section 5).
  • Fair commercial use. You may charge — with honest attribution and disclosure (Sections 2.7, 4.9, 4.10).
